# SevaSetu AI — Problem Statement

## SIH targets
- **SIH26129** — Unified Navigator for Fragmented Government Services
- **SIH26092** — Scheme-Matching and Action Planner for Marginalised Entrepreneurs

## Problem
Government schemes and public services are fragmented across portals, departments, eligibility rules, documents, and application processes. Citizens often know the problem they need to solve, but not the scheme name, department, eligibility requirements, documents, or correct official route.

## User pain
A citizen should not need to understand government administrative structure before receiving help. Searching scheme lists manually creates a high cognitive and information burden and can lead to missed benefits or incorrect applications.

## SevaSetu response
SevaSetu starts with the citizen's problem rather than a scheme name. The system extracts relevant facts, asks only decision-critical follow-up questions, searches verified scheme data, checks eligibility, explains why schemes match, identifies documents, generates an action plan, and points the citizen to the appropriate official government service.

## MVP success condition
One complete journey must work end-to-end:

**Problem → clarification → verified scheme search → eligibility → documents → action plan → official service navigation.**

## Trust boundaries
- SevaSetu is not a government authority.
- Never invent scheme names, rules, benefits, or official URLs.
- Every scheme record should retain a source/reference and verification date.
- Never claim an application was submitted, approved, or processed without a legitimate integration.
- Simulated actions must be explicitly labeled.
